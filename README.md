# Edge Runner 1.2.0 (Forge 1.16.5)
- G: toggle parkour mode (or right click with the Parkour Band). H: show/hide help panel.
- Parkour mode hides the vanilla HUD (hotbar, health, food, armor, xp, air) and shows its own helper.
- Sprint -> momentum. Jump at a wall -> wall run; jump again -> wall jump.
- ONE press of Shift while sprinting -> slide (can hold or release). Works under 1-block-high gaps.
- Hold W + Jump at a ledge (<=3 blocks) -> vault.
- Standing on a fence / wall: slow balancing walk with arms spread.
Font: Tektur (SIL OFL, see assets/edgerunner/font/Tektur-OFL.txt)
